Pricing questions

How does the founding rate work?

The first 15 businesses get our Visibility plan at $199/month instead of $249, or $159/month billed annually, locked in for as long as you stay subscribed. Once the 15 spots fill, Visibility returns to $249/month for new customers and founding members keep their rate.

What is the difference between the Reviews plans and the AI Visibility plans?

Every plan builds your review signal, which is the biggest single body of text AI reads about a local business, so every plan improves your AI visibility. The Reviews plans just do not show you the number. The AI Visibility plans add tracking across Google Maps, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, tell you which businesses are being recommended instead of you, and give you a ranked list of what to fix. Growth is the plan where we do the fixing.

How does the annual discount work?

Choose annual billing and every plan drops 20%: Starter $39/mo, Managed $79/mo, Visibility $199/mo, Growth $399/mo, billed once per year. Founding members get Visibility at $159/mo annual. It is entirely optional, monthly is the default and always will be, and the 30-day money-back guarantee applies to annual plans in full.

How does the 30-day guarantee work?

If you do not want to keep VouchTrack, for any reason at all, email us within 30 days of your first payment and we refund it in full. That applies to monthly and annual plans alike. The optional one-time website build on Growth is billed and delivered separately and sits outside this guarantee.

What counts as a text or an email?

Each review request we send on your behalf counts as one message. A typical customer receives one text or one email after their visit, plus at most one gentle reminder. If you serve 200 customers a month you will use roughly 200 to 400 messages, comfortably inside every plan's allowance.

How do the message sliders work?

Every plan includes a monthly allowance of texts and emails. If your volume is higher, slide to add more: extra texts are $2.50 per 100 and extra emails are $2.50 per 1,000. Add-ons are always billed monthly, even on annual plans, so you can raise or lower them whenever your volume changes.

What if I have more than one location?

$99/month per additional location on any plan except Starter. Each location gets the same features as your base plan, its own review inbox, its own AI visibility tracking, and the same done-for-you setup, billed on the same cycle as your plan.

Why is the Growth plan limited?

Because eight published articles a month plus a full profile build-out is real work, and we would rather cap the number of clients than quietly do a worse job for all of them. If Growth is full we will tell you, and Visibility is available immediately.

Will my price go up later?

No. The rate you join at is the rate you keep. Founding members lock in Visibility at $199/month, or $159/month annual, for as long as they stay subscribed, even after the founding spots fill.

Is there really no contract?

Really. Month to month, cancel with one email, effective at the end of your billing cycle. The annual option is a discount for prepaying, not a contract we hold you to, and it is covered by the same 30-day guarantee.

What happens on the free demo call?

Fifteen minutes. We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity about your market while you watch, show you who they name, pull up your Google profile next to them, and walk you through exactly what we would do about it. If it is not a fit we will tell you that too.
