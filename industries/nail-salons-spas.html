Spa & nail salon owners ask us

My clients book through Instagram — do Google reviews still matter?

Instagram shows your work to people who already follow you. Google reviews win the people who've never heard of you — the "nail salon near me" searchers who become your next regulars. You need both, and only one of them runs on autopilot with VouchTrack.

Will the texts annoy my clients?

Each client receives one friendly request and at most one gentle reminder — that's it. The message is warm, short, and written in your salon's voice. Clients consistently tell owners they appreciated being asked.

We have two locations. Does this work?

Yes — each location gets its own review flow, its own requests, and its own report card, all managed together. Multi-location pricing is simple; ask us on the call.

How AI decides which salon to name

Clients ask about cleanliness and about specific services by name. Assistants answer from what your regulars wrote, so reviews mentioning the service someone is searching for do the heavy lifting.

Three things decide it: your Google Business Profile, your review signals, and what the rest of the web says about you. We show you where you stand on all three, name the salons being recommended in your place, and then do the work.
