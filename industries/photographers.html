Photographers ask us

Most of my bookings come from referrals and Instagram. Do reviews still matter?

Referrals and Instagram start the conversation, but nearly every couple still Googles you before they reach out, and more of them now ask ChatGPT who to hire. Your reviews are the proof that the referral was right. Instagram reach is rented; your Google profile is the one page every couple checks, and you own it.

When should I ask a wedding client for a review?

The day the gallery lands. That is the emotional peak of the whole experience, and it can be months after the wedding itself. VouchTrack times the request to gallery delivery automatically, and follows up gently with couples who meant to write one and got busy.

I shoot weddings, portraits and the occasional brand job. Does this still work?

Yes. Requests go out per client, written in your voice, so the message a bride gets reads differently from the one a family or a marketing manager gets. Weddings, minis, newborns, headshots, commercial work: if a happy client has a phone, it works.

How AI decides which photographer to name

Couples never ask for the nearest photographer. They ask for someone who shoots candid documentary weddings, or someone great with nervous families. Assistants answer from what your clients wrote, so reviews that name a venue, a style or the way you ran the day are worth far more than a bare five stars.

Three things decide it: your Google Business Profile, your review signals, and what the rest of the web says about you. We show you where you stand on all three, name the photographers being recommended in your place, and then do the work.
