How it works in a salon

Client checks out

After their appointment, each client automatically gets a review request by text or email — timed for when they're happiest. A QR code by the register catches walk-ins too.

Reviews roll in

Most happy clients will leave a review when asked — the trick is asking every single time. We do. Your review count starts climbing in week one.

New clients book

When your rating and review count pass the competition, you become the obvious choice for everyone searching in your neighborhood. More calls, more bookings, fuller chairs.

Salon owners ask us

My clients are regulars — do reviews still matter?

Regulars keep the lights on, but growth comes from new faces — and every new client starts with a Google search. Reviews are also your best defense when a stylist leaves and tries to take clients: a strong salon brand outlasts any one chair.

Can requests come from my salon's name?

Yes. Every text and email comes from your salon's name, written in your voice. Clients will never see VouchTrack mentioned anywhere.

What if a client had a bad experience?

Our feedback flow gives unhappy clients a private way to tell you first — so you can fix it with a call or a redo before it becomes a public one-star. And when negative reviews do appear, they get a prompt, professional reply that makes you look good to everyone reading.

How AI decides which salon to name

Clients ask for someone who can handle their specific hair, not for the nearest salon. Assistants answer from what your clients wrote, so reviews naming a service or a stylist are worth far more than a bare five stars.

Three things decide it: your Google Business Profile, your review signals, and what the rest of the web says about you. We show you where you stand on all three, name the salons being recommended in your place, and then do the work.
