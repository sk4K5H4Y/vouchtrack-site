About VouchTrack

Main street deserves the same machine the franchises have

The big chains have marketing teams, enterprise software and agencies making sure every location turns up wherever customers are looking, including inside the AI assistants they now ask. The owner competing with them has a front desk that is supposed to remember to ask for a review. VouchTrack exists to fix that imbalance.

Hi, I'm Akshay Sudarsan

Connect with me on LinkedIn →

I started VouchTrack after seeing the same story repeat everywhere. Genuinely excellent local businesses, the best cut in the neighborhood, the honest mechanic, the dentist patients drive across town for, losing new customers to a competitor with a louder profile. Not a better business. A more findable one.

Then the ground moved again. Customers stopped scrolling a page of links and started asking one question and getting one answer, from Google's AI summary or from ChatGPT directly. Suddenly it was not enough to be on page one. You had to be one of the two or three names the machine says out loud. And almost nobody running a local business has any idea whether they are.

Every tool built for this problem is aimed at national brands with an agency attached. So VouchTrack is built as a service, not a tool: we show you where you actually stand across the assistants, who is being recommended instead of you, and then we do the work. AI drafts and reads at a scale no person could. People handle judgment. Nothing reaches your customers or your profile without a human saying yes.

And because I have sat through enough enterprise software sales calls for one lifetime: our pricing is published on the website, there are no contracts, and your first 30 days are money-back guaranteed. If we are good, you will stay. That is the whole retention strategy.

— Akshay Sudarsan, founder

What we believe

Published pricing

If a company won't tell you the price before a sales call, the price is "whatever they think you'll pay." Ours is on the pricing page.

No locked doors

Month to month, cancel with one email. Annual billing exists only as a discount you choose — never a contract we hold over you.

A person, not a queue

You get a direct line to someone who knows your business by name. Ticket numbers are for the enterprise platforms.

AI with adult supervision

AI drafts, humans approve. Your reputation is too important for autopilot — and too much work for manual.

Main street first

Every AI visibility tool on the market is built for national brands. Ours is built for salons, auto shops, dental offices and restaurants with one location and no marketing department.

Proof over promises

We start by showing you where you stand today, so you can judge us against a number you saw before you paid. There is a calculator to sanity-check the math first, too.

Come say hello

Book a free 15-minute call. You will talk to a person who will ask the assistants about your market, pull up your profile, and give you an honest read, whether or not we end up working together.
