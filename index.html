Three things decide whether AI names you

Every AI answer about a local business is built from the same short list of sources. We work all three. Setup takes one phone call, and you never touch a dashboard unless you want to.

Your profile

Your Google Business Profile is the first structured record any AI reads about you: categories, services, hours, photos, questions answered. We fill in what is missing and keep it current, because a thin profile is a business the machine cannot confidently describe.

Your review signals

Volume, recency, and the actual words your customers use. This is the biggest single thing AI reads about a local business, and the only one that moves every week. We ask every customer after every visit, and reply to everything that comes back.

Your content footprint

AI answers questions, so it favours businesses whose web presence answers questions. On our top plan we publish the pages that make you the obvious answer to what people in your area are actually asking.

Questions owners ask us

Nobody finds my business through ChatGPT. Why does this matter?

That is the honest question, and the honest answer is that it depends on your town and your trade, which is exactly why we start by measuring it rather than assuming. What is not in dispute is the direction: Google now puts an AI answer above the results, and Maps is already a ranked shortlist rather than a list of links. The businesses that get named in those answers are the ones with a complete profile and a deep, recent review history. That work takes months, so the time to start is before it decides your bookings, not after.

How do you actually change what AI says about me?

Three levers. Your Google Business Profile, which is the structured record assistants read first. Your review signals, which are the biggest thing written about you that AI can quote. And your content footprint, the pages on the web that answer what people in your area are asking. Every plan works your review signals. Our top plan works all three, including profile optimisation and published content.

We already ask customers for reviews. Why do we need this?

Asking in person works — when it happens. But on a busy Saturday, nobody remembers. VouchTrack makes sure every single customer gets asked, automatically, at the moment they're happiest. Most businesses see more reviews in their first 60 days with us than in the previous six months of asking manually.

What about negative reviews?

Two things. First, every negative review gets a prompt, professional response — which studies show matters more to future customers than the review itself. Second, our feedback flow gives unhappy customers a private way to reach you first, so problems get solved before they become public.

How fast will I see results?

You get your visibility baseline in the first week, so you know where you stand immediately. Review requests start going out the same week. Review volume usually moves within 30 days, and rating and ranking within 60 to 90 days, depending on how many customers come through your door. AI visibility is the slowest of the three, because assistants take time to pick up changes.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

Then you shouldn't pay for it. Every plan — monthly or annual — comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee: email us within your first 30 days and we refund everything, no questions, no forms.
