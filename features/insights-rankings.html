The neighborhood ranking map

When someone searches "hair salon near me," Google's answer changes street by street. You might be #1 from two blocks away and invisible from the highway exit where new customers actually are. Our ranking map shows your position across a grid of points around your business — green where you're in the top 3, pink where you're not — and how it shifts month over month as your reviews grow.

It's the difference between "I think we're doing okay" and "we're #2 near the mall and it's improving."

Owners ask us

What exactly is in the report?

Your rating and review count trend, every new review and the reply we posted, your neighborhood ranking map with movement since your last report, your competitor comparison, and the AI insights summary. Two minutes to read, forwardable to a business partner.

How does the ranking map work?

We check your Google ranking for your key search terms from a grid of points around your location — as if searching from each spot in the neighborhood — and plot the results on a map. Rerun regularly, so you can watch coverage spread as your reviews grow.

Do I need to log into anything?

No. The report card comes to your email, and alerts come by text or email as things happen. A full dashboard exists behind it all, and some owners enjoy poking around — but plenty of our happiest customers have never logged in once.

Map rank and AI recommendation are not the same race

Fewer than half the businesses that win Google's local map pack also turn up when someone asks an assistant for a recommendation. Tracking one without the other leaves you looking at half the picture and assuming it is the whole thing.
