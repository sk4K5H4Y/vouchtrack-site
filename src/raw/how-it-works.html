From first call to running quietly

The 15-minute call

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity about your market while you watch, and pull up your Google profile next to whoever they named. If VouchTrack is not a fit, we will say so and you keep the analysis.

We set everything up

One onboarding call: we connect your review sites, wire into your booking or invoicing tool, write your message templates in your voice, and set up your website widgets. You approve the wording, and that is your whole job.

Capture experiences

Your review signal starts building

Within your first week every customer gets a friendly request by text, email or WhatsApp after their visit, plus a QR code for your counter. This is the lever that moves fastest, because reviews are the biggest body of text AI can read and quote about you.

Build authority

Every review gets answered

New reviews, good or bad, get a reply within hours: drafted by AI in your voice, approved by a human before it posts. Unhappy customers get a private path to you before they ever reach the stars.

Increase visibility

You watch the needle move

A plain-English report: where you now surface across the assistants and Google Maps, what moved, who is still being named ahead of you, and what we are working on next. Two minutes to read, no dashboard required.

It compounds

Every signal feeds the next. A fuller profile and a deeper review history make you easier for an assistant to describe, being described brings customers, and those customers become more reviews. The gap starts working in your direction for a change.

Owners ask us

How much of my time does this take?

About an hour total to get started: the 15-minute demo call, then a 30 to 45 minute onboarding call where we set everything up together. After that your involvement is reading a two-minute report when it arrives, and occasionally approving a reply if you have asked to sign off on negative reviews personally.

Do I need to install anything or learn a dashboard?

No. Everything runs on our side. There's a dashboard if you ever want to look around, but plenty of our happiest customers have never logged in once.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your Google Business Profile (we'll walk you through granting it; it takes two minutes), a sense of how customers flow through your business, and how you like to sound. We handle the rest.

What if I already have some bad reviews?

Almost everyone does: it's the starting point, not a problem. Old negative reviews get professional replies (readers care more about your response than the review), and the steady flow of new five-stars pushes your average up and the old ones down the page.
